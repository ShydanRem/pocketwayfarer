Build a native embedded HD-2D game slice called Pocket Wayfarer Lanternbound for players wanting short tactical fantasy expeditions. The product is C11/LVGL on a 466x466 circular Waveshare ESP32-S3 AMOLED touch display; mobile is only the plugin schema proxy, not a phone port. This brief describes the implemented slice; device performance and human fun remain unverified.
Goal: Defeat the room-5 Beacon Guardian and keep one collected relic blueprint.
Core mechanics:
1. Tap Play, Depart, choose one of 3 loadouts with left/right touch buttons, and tap Depart. Choose Trail or Pier at branching rooms; The first Trail creates 1 root keeper and Pier 2 ember wisps. Later solo routes draw from root keeper, moss wolf and tide crab; pair routes draw from ember wisp, lantern bat and jade moth. Route previews use copied state and never consume the run RNG. There is no free character movement or jump. Room order is combat, choice event, combat, combat, boss. Each room is one level, exactly 5 levels.
2. In combat, tap a living enemy to select it, then Attack, Skills or Guard. Skills opens 2 choices, costing 6 SP for power strike or 5 SP for loadout support; unavailable skills are disabled. Guard restores 4 SP and halves incoming damage during that enemy turn. Tap the screen or press BOOT to stop the attack spark or parry wave. Attack rotates every 1400ms and expires after 2800ms. Full Good/Great/Perfect windows are 220/140/90ms, with optional 50 percent wider assist. Power strike and basic attack use 0.4/1/1.25/1.5 quality multipliers, without a second automatic critical multiplier. Enemy intent is shown for 650ms before each parry. Parry takes 100/70/45/20 percent damage by quality. Perfect parry adds one resonance up to 3; a power skill at 3 consumes the charges for the loadout's extra effect.
3. Non-boss combat awards 10 gold and, while below the 3-relic cap, offers 2 distinct relics; tap arrows to compare and Choose to keep one, maximum 3. There are 6 authored effects: perfect parry restores 3 SP; wounded targets take +4 damage; Guard prepares +8 damage; enter battle with 12 shield; hits apply 2 burn damage for 2 turns; continuing heals 6 HP. Room 2 offers Hearth (+25 HP or +12 SP) or Mirror (offer 12 HP, requiring HP above 12, for 3 resonance and +8 SP; refuse for +12 SP). Either gives 10 gold. After each completed room, Continue advances one room or Return banks all earned run gold. Boss gives 40 gold including its normal room award. Victory then asks the player to select and keep one owned relic blueprint.
4. Tap II to Pause and Resume to continue. Pause freezes gameplay and timing. PWR flush saves the full encounter, pending action, enemies, RNG, elapsed timing, resources and paused flag. Restart/wake preserves the phase and requires Resume followed by a 650ms preparation countdown. From results Retry resets run HP/SP, timing, route, resonance, relics and unbanked currency while retaining banked profile, options and blueprint collection. BOOT judges timing during attack/parry, closes the skill menu or pauses at decision phases; menu BOOT returns to village.
Win/lose: 1 life per run. Reaching 0 HP is game over and defeat; retry respawns at room 1 with full HP/SP and no hunger/stamina gate. Sentinel starts 120 HP/24 SP, Ember Mage 96/36, Trickster 104/28. The final boss has 176 HP and alternating heavy/double patterns. Defeat banks 75 percent of run gold with loss rounded down. Victory banks all 80 possible gold. Bank transfer is idempotent across reloading.
Score: Gold is the score/currency. Score progression is +10 per cleared non-boss room and +40 for final boss, maximum 80 after 5 levels. HUD shows room out of 5, HP, SP, selected enemy HP and resonance out of 3. A collected blueprint can equip one starting relic before the next run; this occupies one of the 3 relic slots. Collection and discovered bestiary entries persist across retry.
Screens: Start menu with quick Play and optional Customize, Village, Loadout, Route, Combat HUD and skill menu, Pause, Relic comparison, Recovery event, Continue/Return, Blueprint selection, Victory, Game over/defeat with Retry, Collection with Equip/Remove, Map, seven-entry discovery Bestiary, three-page How to Play, Options, native offline Hunt status/collection, and access to Classic mode.
Design: Original pixel-art HD-2D source atlases with two layered-looking baked backgrounds, 3 distinct loadout appearances, 6 ordinary species and a unique brass lighthouse boss with 4 poses each, 6 relic illustrations, licensed DejaVu fonts with Italian accents, native LVGL controls and localized text. Jade/petrol/ivory/amber palette with vermilion danger. Sprites use integer scaling and shared row-wide scale with fixed foot anchors; the boss has its own authored atlas row. Backgrounds are 233x233 at 2x. Main touch actions are at least 72px tall; controls fit the circular screen. Reduced-motion suppresses ambient pose cycling.
Sound: No audio is implemented because physical audio hardware has not been verified. Essential feedback works silently.
How it should feel: An enemy announces its intent for 650ms before parry, each relic states its numeric effect, and retry returns to full resources in one results-screen action. Target 3-5 minute sessions and input feedback below 100ms are future on-device/human measurements, not certified outcomes.
Build ONLY this bounded slice in the existing native project; preserve the old hero, inventory and Classic mode. Write CRC save v3 while migrating v1 and v2. Classic encounters take a pre-entry transaction snapshot: full restart restores that snapshot and reoffers the encounter; paid results and stairs reconstruct navigation without paying twice. Classic screen power cycling freezes timing and gives a 650ms preparation countdown. Gold quest fractions persist across small payouts, morale agrees across tick chunk sizes, unavailable social quests are filtered and migrated, and a full equipment bag leaves idle loot atomically unclaimed. Provide an optional Linux SDL2 interactive host of the same native LVGL code: mouse is touch, Space/Enter is BOOT, P is power, F5 saves/reloads. No multiplayer, payment, ads, 3D renderer, full class-art replacement or phone/browser port.
Done when: 1) Real LVGL touch and BOOT controls complete all 5 levels and reach victory, defeat, retreat and retry. 2) Pause/save/reload/resume retains the exact enemies, action and elapsed timing; gold cannot be collected twice. 3) Model tests cover all 3 loadouts, all six starting relics, all seven enemies, timing assist and v1/v2 save migration; audit regressions exercise real award and navigation paths. 4) UI screenshots are captured at native 466x466 with circular mask, Italian accents and English. A generic 375px phone-width check does not apply to this firmware; the desktop window letterboxes its 466px logical surface and preserves hit positions when resized. 5) The native ESP32 build fits its 6MiB app partition. Physical touch, frame time, battery and human engagement remain release gates.
