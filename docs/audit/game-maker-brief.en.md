Build a game prototype called "Pocket Wayfarer: Lantern Expeditions" for players who enjoy short tactical RPG sessions and collecting original fantasy heroes.
Platform: The actual product is native C11/LVGL 9.4 firmware for Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75, circular 466x466 touch screen with BOOT and PWR buttons. The plugin's mobile platform selection is only a touch-layout proxy; no browser/mobile migration is requested. Everything below is a proposed vertical slice, not implemented behavior.
Goal: Complete one expedition through 5 rooms, including a final boss, and bring home one selected relic that changes the hero's build. Target session duration 3-5 minutes, a target to measure rather than a timeout.
Core mechanics:
1. Route choice: Tap one of 2 connected, labeled room options at branching rooms to advance exactly 1 room; room 5 has one fixed boss node. A room resolves through combat or a 2-option event; preview enemy intent or reward/risk before selection. For browser review only, Tab selects actionable buttons and Enter activates them; no character movement or jumping exists.
2. Combat: Tap a living enemy to select it, then tap Attack or one of 2 equipped skills. Attack and damaging skills open a timing ring; tap anywhere or press BOOT once to strike. Proposed full timing windows: Good 220 ms, Great 140 ms, Perfect 90 ms at a fixed 1400 ms rotation; resolve once per action, with a 2800 ms timeout causing a weak strike. Proposed damage multipliers 0.4/1.0/1.25/1.5 by quality, with no extra critical multiplier stacked on Perfect. Each hero turn is followed by each surviving enemy, at most 2 in ordinary fights.
3. Defense and mastery: Before an enemy attack, show a labeled attack intent for at least 650 ms, then a closing wave with the same 220/140/90 ms full windows. Miss/Good/Great/Perfect take 100/70/45/20 percent of incoming damage. Perfect adds 1 resonance charge; at 3 charges, one equipped skill may consume all charges for its archetype effect: Sentinel gains a shield worth 20 percent of max HP for 2 turns, Ember Mage applies burn for 2 turns worth 5 percent enemy max HP per turn (1 percent for bosses), Trickster immediately repeats one basic hit at 70 percent power. Charges then reset to 0. No damage immunity or counterattack on every perfect.
4. Rewards and risk: After a won non-boss combat, show 2 temporary relic options and tap exactly 1; maximum 3 temporary relics. No temporary relic is awarded after the final boss. Room order is combat, event, combat, combat or elite, then boss. Events present 2 explicit outcomes. At each resolved room, Continue advances while Return banks earned currency and ends the run. Final boss victory banks earned currency and allows keeping 1 relic blueprint. Defeat keeps banked progression and loses only 25 percent of unbanked run currency.
5. Pause, save and retry: BOOT on route returns to the previous menu; during timing it judges the action. A visible Pause button freezes simulation, timing and animations, then Resume continues. PWR saves and suspends the current battle and run; wake restores the same phase with a new 650 ms ready countdown. From results, Retry resets run-only HP/SP, charges, temporary relics, route and unbanked currency and starts room 1 within 2 taps. Persistent unlocked cosmetics/blueprints remain.
Win/lose: One life per expedition. Hero HP reaching 0 loses and respawns in town with full HP and no stamina gate; defeating the room-5 boss wins. No arbitrary real-time deadline. New run restores full HP and the chosen loadout. Free retreat is available after every resolved room.
Scoring & progression: Display rooms completed out of 5, current HP/SP, resonance 0-3, run currency and banked currency. Each cleared room gives 10 run currency; boss gives 30 additional currency, maximum base total 80. Permanent progression unlocks sidegrade blueprints and cosmetics, not automatic stat inflation. One region, 3 selectable archetypes (Sentinel, Ember Mage, Trickster) with 2 equipped skills each, 6 ordinary enemy species, 1 boss with 2 telegraphed attack patterns, 6 authored relics and 2 choice events. No random legendary drop table in this slice. Offline hunting is capped at 12 hours and gives common crafting materials only; a return is never penalized for elapsed absence. Stamina and hunger cannot block or weaken active expeditions.
Screens: Quick start/hero choice, town with one primary Depart action, route, combat HUD, relic choice, pause/resume, victory, defeat with Retry, and options with sound on/off and assist timing +50 percent. Optional character cosmetics follow the first expedition instead of blocking the first fight.
Design: User-selected complete HD-2D pixel-art redesign: original authored crisp sprites, layered diorama backgrounds, baked lantern lighting and restrained LVGL effects. Proposed 233x233 background grid at 2x to the native 466x466 display; hero frames up to 56x72 at 2x with stable foot pivots. Ink/petrol/jade/ivory/amber palette, vermilion plus shape/text for danger. Rebuild character art, environments, symbols and typography coherently; do not retain the old rainbow button theme. The current generated board is visual concept only, not runtime assets or measured hardware performance. Keep essential text/actions in a central safe area verified against the circular mask, with minimum 72px primary touch targets (about 6.9mm at the nominal 1.75-inch display diameter); secondary targets start at 52px and require usability testing; show shape/text cues as well as color.
Sound: Optional brief attack/parry/relic sounds if supported by the existing board's verified audio hardware; Mute option. Do not assume a speaker exists.
How it should feel: Measured input feedback within 100 ms on hardware, first playable battle within 45 seconds, ordinary fight target 15-35 seconds, boss 40-60 seconds, resume and retry predictable.
Build ONLY this slice in the existing native project; do not rewrite drivers, delete existing class/content tables or break old saves. Freeze multiplayer, additional regions, class trees, ads, payments and leaderboards. Version any changed save format and retain/migrate the existing profile. Numeric values above are proposed starting values for human playtests.
Done when: 1) All 3 archetypes can complete room 5 through actual touch/BOOT controls. 2) Victory, HP-zero loss, retreat and Retry are each reachable and preserve the stated currency rules. 3) Pause/resume, PWR suspend/wake, power-cut save recovery and 12-hour offline collection preserve correct state. 4) Key actions and text fit the 466px circular mask and pass a separate 375px touch-layout proxy with no page scrolling; proxy success is not hardware certification. 5) On-device input latency is measured and 5 new human players attempt the slice; target at least 4 understand the timing and at least 3 voluntarily replay once, to be confirmed rather than claimed.
Cadence planning: proposed average room budget 36 seconds (5 x 36 = 180 seconds), including choices, with up to 120 additional seconds for boss and loadout/result screens. This is a pacing budget, not an enemy wave model. No fire rate, jumping or movement metric applies.
